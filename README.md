# Energy Transport in Conductors: A Field-Based View of EMI in Avionics Wiring

**Jesús Alberto Perea García** — Mechatronics Engineering, IEST Anáhuac

📄 **[Read the PDF](Perea-Garcia_2026_Energy-Transport-in-Conductors.pdf)**  ·  DOI: _pending_

## Abstract

Engineering intuition often treats wires as simple pipes for energy, an analogy that fails to explain the gap between the millimeter-per-second drift of electrons and the near light-speed propagation of signals. Using the Drude model, surface-charge boundary conditions and the Poynting vector, this technical note shows that energy propagates through the electromagnetic fields surrounding a conductor rather than through the charge carriers. The field-centric view is then applied to electromagnetic interference (EMI) in avionics wiring, interpreting twisted pairs and coaxial shielding as geometric tools that reshape and confine the field volume to prevent crosstalk.
